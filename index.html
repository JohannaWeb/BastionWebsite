Philosophy

Infrastructure for people the internet was built to exclude.

The modern web stack is controlled by three companies. Your identity lives on their servers. Your messages pass through their pipes. Your browser runs their code.

Bastion is a bet that this doesn't have to be true. A full computing stack — from the kernel to the browser to the social layer — where every component is open, auditable, and owned by the user.

Built in Porto, Portugal. Funded by nobody. Shipped anyway.
